Aeronautical Decision Making and Hazardous Attitudes.

Aeronautical Decision Making (ADM) (PHAK Chapter 2)
- Systematic approach that is used to determine the best course of action
- Aviate – Navigate – Communicate (Order of Priority)
- PAVE Checklist – Identify Hazards & Personal Minimums
  a. Pilot (IMSAFE)
  b. Aircraft (AVIATE, ARROWPEC, Weight & Balance, Performance)
  c. EnVironment (NEWKRAFT, Pre-flight Planning)
  d. External Pressure ("get-there-itis", impress someone, meetings)
- 3P Model – used during all phases of flight
  a. Perceive – the given set of circumstances for a flight
  b. Process – by evaluating their impact on flight safety
  c. Perform – by implementing the best course of action
- DECIDE Model
  a. Detect the problem
  b. Estimate the severity
  c. Choose a course of action
  d. Identify solutions
  e. Do the course of action
  f. Evaluate the course of action

Hazardous Attitudes & Antidotes (PHAK Chapter 2)
- Anti-Authority
  a. "Don't tell me."
  b. Follow the rule. They are usually right.
- Impulsivity
  a. "Do it quickly."
  b. Not so fast. Think first.
- Invulnerability
  a. "It won't happen to me"
  b. It could happen to me.
- Macho
  a. "I can do it."
  b. Taking chances is foolish.
- Resignation
  a. "What's the use?"
  b. I'm not helpless. I can make a difference
