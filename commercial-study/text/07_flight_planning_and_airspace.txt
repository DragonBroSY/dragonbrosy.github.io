Classes of Airspace (PHAK Chapter 15)
Airspace diagram labels: Class A, 18,000ft to FL600. Class E. Class G. Class D. Class C. Class B. 700ft AGL. 1,200ft AGL. Nontowered airport with instrument approach. Nontowered airport with no instrument approach.

Class A
- Entry Requirements: ATC Clearance
- Minimum Pilot Qualifications: Instrument Rating
- Equipment Required: IFR Equipped
- Traffic Advisories: Yes
Class B
- Entry Requirements: ATC Clearance
- Minimum Pilot Qualifications: Private
- Equipment Required: Two-Way Radio, ADSB-Out, Transponder & Automatic Altitude Reporting
- Traffic Advisories: Yes
Class C
- Entry Requirements: Prior Two-Way Communications
- Minimum Pilot Qualifications: Student
- Equipment Required: Two-Way Radio, ADSB-Out, Transponder & Automatic Altitude Reporting
- Traffic Advisories: Yes
Class D
- Entry Requirements: Prior Two-Way Communications
- Minimum Pilot Qualifications: Student
- Equipment Required: Two-Way Radio
- Traffic Advisories: Workload Permitting
Class E
- Entry Requirements: None For VFR
- Minimum Pilot Qualifications: Student
- Equipment Required: No Specific Requirement
- Traffic Advisories: Workload Permitting
Class G
- Entry Requirements: None
- Minimum Pilot Qualifications: Student
- Equipment Required: No Specific Requirement
- Traffic Advisories: Workload Permitting

VFR Cloud Clearance Requirements
- At or above 10,000ft MSL, up to but not including 18,000ft MSL: 5 SM Visibility, 1,000ft Above, 1,000ft Below, 1 SM Horizontal
- Below 10,000ft MSL, Class B, C, D, E: 3 SM Visibility, 1,000ft Above, 500ft Below, 2,000ft Horizontal
  - Class B: 3SM Clear of Clouds
- Below 10,000ft MSL and more than 1,200ft AGL, Class G: 1 SM, 1,000ft Above, 500ft Below, 2,000ft Horizontal
  - Night: 3SM - 1,000ft Above - 500ft Below - 2,000ft Horizontal
- At or below 1,200ft AGL, Class G: 1SM Clear of Clouds

Special Use Airspace (PHAK Chapter 15)
M.C.P.R.A.W.N.
a. Military Operation Areas (MOA)
b. Controlled Firing Areas (CFA)
c. Prohibited Areas (P-204)
d. Restricted Areas (R-2310A)
e. Alert Areas (A-631)
f. Warning Areas (W-518)
g. National Security Areas (NSA)

Military Operation Areas (MOA)
- Defined lateral & vertical limits
- Separates military training from IFR traffic
- VFR traffic does need prior authorization
- i.e. "OUTLAW MOA"
Prohibited Areas
- Defined lateral & vertical limits
- Established for security or national welfare
- Flight of aircraft is prohibited
- i.e. "P-204"
Alert Areas
- Exercise caution
- Inform nonparticipating pilots of high volume of pilot training or aerial activity
- i.e. "A-631"
Restricted Areas
- Defined lateral & vertical limits
- Allowed to fly through with prior authorization from controlling agency
- Flight hazardous by nonparticipating aircraft
- i.e. "R-2310A"
National Security Areas (NSAs)
- Defined lateral & vertical limits
- Established for increased safety & security of ground facilities
- May be temorarily prohibited airspace
- Pilots are asked to voluntarily avoid overflying
Warning Areas
- Similar in nature to restricted areas, however US government does not have sole jurisdiction
- Extends from 3NM outward from the coast of the US
- Warns nonparticipating pilots of potential danger
Temporary Flight Restrictions (TFR)
- Established by FDC NOTAMs
- Defined by time periord, statute miles, and affected altitudes
Controlled Firing Areas (CFA)
- No chart markings
- Spotter on ground responsible for suspending activities
