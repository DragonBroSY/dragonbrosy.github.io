II.L Water and Seaplane Characteristics, Seaplane Bases, Maritime Rules, and Aids to Marine Navigation (ASES, AMES)

Task L. Water and Seaplane Characteristics, Seaplane Bases, Maritime Rules, and Aids to Marine Navigation (ASES, AMES)
References: AIM; Chart Supplements; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-9, FAA-H-8083-23, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM; USCG Navigation Rules
Objective: To determine the applicant understands water and seaplane characteristics, seaplane bases, maritime rules, and aids to marine navigation, can apply that knowledge, manage associated risks, demonstrate appropriate skills, and provide effective instruction.

Knowledge: The applicant demonstrates instructional knowledge by describing and explaining:
K1. Characteristics of a water surface as affected by features, such as:
    a. Size and location
    b. Protected and unprotected areas
    c. Surface wind
    d. Direction and strength of water current
    e. Floating and partially submerged debris
    f. Sandbars, islands, and shoals
    g. Vessel traffic and wakes
    h. Direction and height of waves
    i. Other characteristics specific to the area
K2. Float and hull construction, and its effect on seaplane performance.
K3. Causes of porpoising and skipping, and the pilot action needed to prevent or correct these occurrences.
K4. How to locate and identify seaplane bases on charts or in directories.
K5. Operating restrictions at various bases.
K6. Right-of-way, steering, and sailing rules pertinent to seaplane operation.
K7. Marine navigation aids, such as buoys, beacons, lights, sound signals, and range markers.
K8. Naval vessel protection zones.
K9. No wake zones.

Risk Management: The applicant explains and teaches how to identify and manage risk associated with:
R1. Local conditions.
R2. Impact of marine traffic.
R3. Right-of-way and sailing rules pertinent to seaplane operations.
R4. Limited services and assistance available at seaplane bases.

Skills: The applicant demonstrates and simultaneously explains how to:
S1. Explain how float and hull construction can affect seaplane performance.
S2. Describe how to correct for porpoising and skipping.
S3. Identify marine navigation aids.
S4. Describe correct right-of-way, steering, and sailing operations.
S5. Assess the water surface characteristics for the proposed flight.
S6. Identify restrictions at local seaplane bases.
